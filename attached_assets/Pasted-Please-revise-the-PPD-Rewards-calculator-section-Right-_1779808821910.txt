Please revise the PPD Rewards calculator section.

Right now the calculator only shows estimated rewards based on monthly eligible processing volume. Since the public merchant reward rate is small by itself, the section should feel more referral-focused and more exciting for merchants.

Keep the processing volume slider because I like it, but update the calculator so it includes both:

1. Monthly eligible processing volume
2. Estimated approved referrals per month

The calculator should show a combined estimated monthly value:
Estimated Monthly Rewards = processing reward estimate + referral reward estimate

Use this public-facing logic:
- Processing reward estimate: eligible monthly volume × 0.01%
- Referral reward estimate: approved referrals × configurable referral bonus amount

For the public page, use a sample referral bonus of $75 per approved referral, but do not hard-code it in a way that is difficult to change later. Make it a constant/config value near the calculator component, such as REFERRAL_BONUS_ESTIMATE = 75.

Example outputs:
- $25,000 monthly eligible volume + 2 approved referrals = about $152.50/month
- $50,000 monthly eligible volume + 5 approved referrals = about $380/month
- $100,000 monthly eligible volume + 5 approved referrals = about $385/month
- $250,000 monthly eligible volume + 10 approved referrals = about $775/month

Important:
Do not make the page promise these exact amounts. This needs to feel like an estimate and not a guaranteed payout.
